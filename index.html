Questions, answered

Do I need to know anything about AI or code?

No. Our prompt is fill-in-the-blank: type in your services, service area and contact details, and the AI customizes your chosen template. Prefer your own design? The same prompt works from scratch.

Can I change a template?

Yes, that’s the idea. Change the colors, photos, services and wording as much as you like, and we’ll polish whatever you send back. Think of it as a head start, not a cookie cutter.

What counts as an “unlimited minor edit”?

Routine text swaps, image updates, or up to 2 hours of structural work each month. New pages or a full redesign are quoted as a separate project.

Why is there a minimum term?

We build your full custom site up front, at no upfront build cost. The minimum term, or the optional setup fee, covers that work.

Do you manage social media?

Your Google Business Profile posts are automated in every plan. Hands-on social media management is available as an add-on.
